Syfter Authors
==============

Syfter started as a vibe-coded project to manage SBOMs and perform
vulnerability management exploration (i.e. a new vulnerability shows up, am
I affected? which product?).  It was born out of frustration with existing
tools.  The initial 0.9.0.1 version was a proof of concept, that was
further extended to work in an enterprise, production environment.  It's
still WIP but promising!

While initially put together by Vincent Danen, it has been further extended
by Christopher Lusk and turned into a useful project.  Syfter would not be
where it is today were it not for Christopher's efforts.
